Rating Scales Reference

Quality Scale (1-5):

  • 1 = Very poor (frustrating / confusing / looks unfinished)
  • 2 = Poor (several issues, feels clunky)
  • 3 = OK (works, but nothing special / some friction)
  • 4 = Good (clear, smooth, enjoyable)
  • 5 = Excellent (delightful, polished, would happily use)

Agreement Scale (1-5):

  • 1 = Strongly disagree
  • 2 = Disagree
  • 3 = Neutral / unsure
  • 4 = Agree
  • 5 = Strongly agree

Likelihood Scale (1-5):

  • 1 = Very unlikely
  • 2 = Unlikely
  • 3 = Maybe
  • 4 = Likely
  • 5 = Very likely
