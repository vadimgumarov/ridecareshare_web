What is RideCareShare?

The world's first donation-powered airport carpooling app that turns travel into support for communities.


  • No money exchanges hands between passenger and driver — we call this a "connection" to emphasize it's not a transactional relationship
  • Drivers post upcoming trips to/from airports
  • Passengers browse and join rides that match their schedule
  • Minimum $8 donation goes directly to the charity the driver supports — passengers can choose to donate more

Key Features

  • Ride Matching - Find rides by airport, date, and direction
  • Charitable Giving - Drivers select charities to support
  • Real-time Updates - Live notifications for ride requests
  • Verified Users - Phone, email, and identity verification
  • In-App Chat - Real-time messaging via websocket, nothing saved, no PII stored
  • Location Sharing - Real-time via websocket only, never stored
  • Rating System - Rate your experience after rides
